AI-Based Early Detection of Neurological Disorders using EEG

Project Abstract

Neurological disorders such as Epilepsy, Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), and Alzheimer’s disease affect brain functioning and significantly reduce quality of life. Early detection of these disorders is essential for effective treatment and prevention.

This project proposes an EEG-based machine learning framework for detecting neurological disorders. EEG signals are preprocessed using FIR filtering and Independent Component Analysis (ICA) to remove noise and artifacts. Meaningful features such as Bandpower and Shannon entropy are extracted to represent brain activity patterns.

The system uses ensemble machine learning models such as XGBoost and LightGBM along with a hybrid CNN–LSTM deep learning architecture to classify EEG signals accurately. In addition to detection, the system provides possible causes and precautionary guidance to support early medical consultation and patient awareness.

Limitations of Existing Systems

Noise Sensitivity

EEG signals are low amplitude and highly affected by noise and artifacts.

Limited Accuracy

Traditional ML models struggle to detect complex neurological patterns.

Handcrafted Features

Heavy dependence on manual feature extraction misses hidden EEG patterns.

Dataset Dependency

Existing systems often work only for specific datasets or single disorders.

Advantages of Proposed System

High Detection Accuracy

Combines ensemble ML models and CNN-LSTM deep learning for better classification.

Advanced Preprocessing

FIR filters and ICA remove noise and improve EEG signal quality.

Multiple Disorder Detection

Supports detection of Epilepsy, Autism Spectrum Disorder, and Alzheimer’s disease.

Patient Guidance

Provides possible causes and precautionary recommendations for patients.

Impact on Society

Neurological disorders affect millions of people worldwide and often remain undiagnosed in early stages. Automated EEG-based detection systems can assist doctors and healthcare providers by identifying abnormal brain patterns quickly and accurately.

This system acts as an intelligent decision-support tool that helps in early diagnosis, improves patient awareness, and encourages timely medical consultation. By integrating artificial intelligence with neurological signal analysis, the system contributes to better healthcare outcomes and improved quality of life.
